Database: Transcriptome of Irritable Bowel Syndrome (TIBS)

About this database

We have integrated four public expression microarray data (320 samples) from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) and ArrayExpress databases into an online database called Transcriptome of Irritable Bowel Syndrome (TIBS). The gene expression change in IBS patients compared to healthy volunteers or ulcerative colitis (UC) patients in jejunum, sigmoid colon, rectum, and descending colon can be queried by gene symbols. Users can compare gene expression levels of IBS patients across these tissues. Sex difference of gene expression in IBS patients was also shown in the database. The database supports genes associated with multiple probe IDs, allowing users to retrieve all available documents for a given gene.

How to use:

Single gene search: DUOX2 (enable Fuzzy search to match partial names like DUOX → DUOX1, DUOX2)

Multiple gene search: DUOX2; PPARG; HSPA6 (exact match only)

Reference: TIBS: A web database to browse gene expression in irritable bowel syndrome, Journal of Theoretical Biology, 2014, 354: 48-53
